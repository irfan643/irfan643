### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Developer from Pakistan, passionate about turning ideas into 
real, working products. I enjoy owning a project end-to-end — from designing 
the system architecture to writing clean APIs to deploying the final product. 
My journey started with curiosity about how apps work behind the scenes, and 
it's grown into building full platforms used by real people.

During my internship at G7 Technologies, I built an entire LMS (Learning 
Management System) from scratch, including 20+ REST APIs, and also 
developed an internal Salary Management System for the company. Outside of 
work, I built MindCare, a mental health & wellness mobile app using React 
Native, Expo, and Firebase — something I'm genuinely proud of because it 
solves a problem I care about.

I'm currently sharpening my skills in System Design, Docker, and CI/CD, and 
I'm always open to collaborating on projects that challenge me to grow.
